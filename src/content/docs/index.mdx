## Why Vitrine

<CardGrid>
	<Card title="Beneath your icons" icon="laptop">
		Widgets live between the wallpaper and the desktop icons, exactly where they belong. Your icons stay
		visible and clickable.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Still interactive" icon="approve-check">
		Clicks and scrolls that miss an icon reach the widget underneath, so buttons, lists and toggles just
		work.
	</Card>
	<Card title="A grid, not a theme" icon="puzzle">
		Place, resize and snap widgets on a grid, on every monitor. Run several instances of the same widget,
		each with its own settings.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Native and frugal" icon="rocket">
		A Rust engine with its own renderer, no browser engine. Vitrine redraws only what changes and pauses
		when the desktop is hidden or you are on battery saver.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Write widgets in JSX" icon="pencil">
		Build widgets with JSX and SolidJS fine-grained reactivity through `@vitrine/sdk`, then share them as
		a single `.vwidget` file.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Permissions, not surprises" icon="setting">
		Every widget declares what it needs (system metrics, specific network hosts) and gets nothing else.
	</Card>
</CardGrid>
